# Sacred Heart School Homepage

Homepage for "Sacred Heart School,"

a co-ed, English-medium school affiliated to CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education), located in

Mauza Nichlaul, Pargana Tilpur, Mandi Road, Nichlaul, Maharajganj District, Uttar Pradesh, 273305.

STYLE DIRECTION

- Clean, bright, welcoming — soft rounded cards, generous whitespace
- Warm color palette (navy/maroon primary + gold accent)
- Established, trustworthy feel
- High-quality photos of campus, classrooms, labs, students
- Fully responsive, mobile-first layout

SECTIONS (single homepage, in this order)

1. Header / Navbar
   - Logo (placeholder) + "Sacred Heart School"
   - Nav: Home, About Us, Academics, Boarding, Admissions, Gallery, Contact
   - "Admissions Open" / "Enquire Now" CTA button

2. Hero Section
   - Full-width campus/students photo placeholder
   - Affiliated to CBSE badge
   - CTAs: "Apply for Admission" and "Explore Boarding Life"

3. Quick Highlights Strip (icons + short label)
   "Affiliated to CBSE" | "Full Boarding Available" | "30:1 Student-Teacher Ratio"

4. About / Welcome Section
   - Warm welcome message about academic guidance, character building, and individual attention
   - Supporting campus photo

5. Why Choose Us (4-column icon grid)
   - CBSE Curriculum, Boys' & Girls' Hostels, Smart Classrooms, Rich Co-Curricular Life
   - 1-2 line description each

6. Academic Life
   - Overview of CBSE curriculum, English medium, April-March session
   - Classroom photo placeholder

7. Boarding Life
   - Highlight separate boys' and girls' hostels
   - Warm framing: "A home away from home" — daily routine, pastoral care

8. Life at Sacred Heart (staff & students gallery)
   - Photo grid mixing staff and student moments
   - Caption: "Meet the people who make Sacred Heart School a family"

9. Facilities Grid
   Library, Science Lab, Computer Lab, Auditorium, Cafeteria/Canteen,
   WiFi & Smart Classes, Medical Room, CCTV Security, Transportation

10. Co-Curricular & Sports
    - Art & Craft, Dance, Drama, Music, Debate, Gardening, Picnics &
      Excursions, Yoga, Indoor & Outdoor Sports

11. Testimonials
    - Parent and student quote cards

12. Admissions CTA Banner
    - "Admissions Open" with enquiry form

13. Footer
    - Address: Mauza Nichlaul, Pargana Tilpur, Mandi Road, Nichlaul, Maharajganj District, Uttar Pradesh, 273305
    - Phone: +91 9076821500, Email: shsthoothibari@gmail.com
    - Google Map embed of Nichlaul location
    - Copyright line

FUNCTIONAL NOTES

- All photo placeholders clearly swappable

- Enquiry form: front-end only for now, fields as above

- Tone: warm, family-run heritage school — not corporate, not a boarding-school

  chain aesthetic

- Boarding life deserves visual and narrative weight — it's a genuine

  differentiator, not just a bullet point

- Only build the homepage now; leave nav links ready to wire up later
